# Coretext Organization Growth

## Status
- Focus: making the current Coretext surface understandable and valuable through the dashboard, package mirror, and clear docs.
- **Hypothesis:** A "beautiful" scaffolding framework (like GitNexus graph) will drive adoption even if less scientifically accurate than raw D-SDD.
## Market Positioning
- The product angle is a file-centric, deterministic knowledge base for agent workflows.
- The differentiator is the combination of graph routing, session traces, and durable review rules.
- **Competitor Comparison:** 
    - **Traditional:** Jira, Slack, Notion (Stateless, High Friction).
    - **New Wave:** Abacus.ai, OpenClaw (Heavy, Outsourcing-centric).
    - **The Gap:** Minimalist, file-centric, stateful knowledge infrastructure.
    
## PMF Strategies
- Use the dashboard to make the graph legible.
- Use packaging and setup scripts to lower adoption friction.
- Keep the rule and note system aligned so the story stays credible.
