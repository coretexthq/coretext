Manual Verification Walkthrough

To verify the interactive user experience in the main portal, follow these manual test cases:

  1. URL-Driven Filter State:
    • Go to the Main Portal Page.
    • Enter a query in text search, choose a district (e.g. Downtown), check some amenities, and click a sorting option.
    • Look at your browser's address bar. Notice that the URL query parameters automatically update (e.g., ?q=Modern&district=Downtown&amenities=pool...).
    • Copy the URL, open a new browser tab, and paste it. Verify that the filters are pre-populated and the exact same listings are loaded.
  2. Saved Searches Integration:
    • Select a renter (e.g., Dave Jones) in the renter selector dropdown.
    • Modify filters, then click the Save Search button. Verify a success toast notification appears.
    • Look in the Saved Searches panel. Verify your saved search is now listed under Dave Jones' searches.
    • Change all filter values or reset filters, then click the Load button on the saved search. Verify the URL updates and the search filters are fully restored.
  3. Price Quote Calculator & Booking Validation:
    • Click a listing card (e.g., Sleek Modern Loft in Downtown) to open the details modal.
    • Notice the list of blackout dates (e.g., July 10 to July 15) and approved bookings (e.g., July 1 to July 5).
    • Enter arrival as 2026-07-06 and departure as 2026-07-09. Verify the price quote calculator appears showing 3 nights and calculating the total price.
    • Fill in name/email and click Submit Booking Request. Verify a green success alert shows with your request ID.
    • Now close the modal and click it again. Verify the new dates now appear under "Confirmed Bookings".
    • Try submitting a booking for overlapping dates (e.g. July 2 to July 4). Verify the submission fails with a clear validation error about overlapping bookings.
