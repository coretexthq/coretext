# LLM as the Language Module and the Cognitive Infrastructure of Coretext

Ev Fedorenko’s research proposes that the human brain contains a specialized "language network"—a "glorified parser" or "mindless language processor"—that is physically distinct from the networks responsible for high-level reasoning. An LLM, in its raw form, is essentially this language module floating without the rest of the cognitive hardware (logic, social cognition, world-modeling).

While the "Actual Intelligence" is the transformer architecture (Attention Mechanism) converging toward brain-like geometries, language serves as the primary substrate of training and the only window into reality for the model. 

The work of building Coretext is the construction of an **Extra-Linguistic Cognitive System** for the LLM. It mimics the brain's modularity by providing the missing pieces: the **Multiple Demand Network** (Z3/Formal Logic), the **Hippocampus** (SurrealDB/Episodic Memory), and the **Neocortex** (Knowledge Graph/Semantic Memory). This infrastructure allows the "Language Intelligence" of the LLM to become "Functional Intelligence" by providing state, verifiability, and persistent world-modeling.
