## Summary
Refined the Coretext v2 architecture by abandoning complex, multimodal SurrealDB graphs and Hebbian learning in favor of a brutally minimalist, deterministic SQLite index mapped to flat text files (CHANGELOG.md). Accepted that the Planner agent acts as a necessary but lossy organizational manager, curating JIT context for the Executor, to bridge current model context limits without building "Heavy Slop" harnesses.

## Problems & Solutions
- **Problem**: Model's finite context window requires dynamic attention management, but injecting massive codebases or complex SurrealDB graphs introduces "Semantic Rot" and unnecessary complexity.
  - **Solution**: Adopt a minimalist SQLite index mapping file paths to specific lines in `CHANGELOG.md` or `ARCHITECTURE.md` to deliver targeted Just-In-Time (JIT) context.
- **Problem**: Treating AI agents like single human brains ignores the lossy nature of intent transfer over time and across different model instances.
  - **Solution**: Shift to an Organizational Metaphor. Treat the Planner as a "Manager" that distills Executor ("Employee") experiences into a centralized, curated text ledger (`CHANGELOG.md`), acknowledging the Planner as a single point of failure that must be rigorously constrained by prompts.
- **Problem**: The market is saturated with complex, heavy agent harnesses (like ClaudeKit, BMad) that act as "commercialized slop," hiding the fundamental nature of the context problem behind buzzwords.
  - **Solution**: Reject "Harness Engineering." Treat the harness as a temporary, minimal scaffolding (SQLite + Planner loop) designed to be easily discardable as foundational models solve context and attention natively (e.g., Attention Residuals research).
